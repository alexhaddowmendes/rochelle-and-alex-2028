Getting there

Flying from the UK. Airlines that fly from the UK to the Philippines include Qatar Airways, Saudia and Singapore Airlines.

When to book. Start searching around April 2027 for cheaper flights. Expect to pay roughly £600–£1,000.

Layovers. Allow at least 2 hours between flights. You'll need to get off the plane, go through security and find your gate, and it gives your suitcase time to transfer with you.

Timing. Book a morning flight out, so you land in the Philippines in the morning or early afternoon. For the journey home, a late-afternoon or evening flight from the Philippines gets you back to England in the morning.

Manila to the venue. We'll be arranging travel for everyone from Manila to the venue, so you only need to get yourself to Manila.

Dress code and weather

Beach formal. Think dressed up, but made for sand and sea breeze: light, breathable fabrics like linen, cotton or chiffon. Please skip white so the couple stand out. Flat or block-heel shoes are best, as stilettos sink into the sand.

Weather. April is the tail end of the dry season in Zambales: expect hot, sunny days around 33°C, with little rain and warm evenings near 24°C. Bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat for daytime, and a light layer for the breeze after sunset.
