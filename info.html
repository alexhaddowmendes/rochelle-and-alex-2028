Dress code and weather

Beach formal. Think dressed up, but made for sand and sea breeze: light, breathable fabrics like linen, cotton or chiffon. Please skip white so the couple stand out. Flat or block-heel shoes are best, as stilettos sink into the sand.

Weather. April is the tail end of the dry season in Zambales: expect hot, sunny days around 33°C, with little rain and warm evenings near 24°C. Bring sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat for daytime, and a light layer for the breeze after sunset.
